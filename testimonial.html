I had an amazing experience at Khanna Eye Centre and couldn’t be happier with my decision to undergo IPCL (Implantable Phakic Contact Lens) surgery there.

Since I had retinal holes due to my high myopia, he first advised me to undergo barrage laser treatment before proceeding with the surgery. I really appreciated his thorough and honest approach, as it showed that my eye health was always the top priority.

Dr. Ankita Khanna has been exceptional throughout this entire journey. I had a high refractive error in both eyes (spherical -6 and cylindrical -4), and she took the utmost care during every step—from the pre-operative evaluation to the surgeries on both eyes. The results have honestly been life-changing. My vision is now 6/6, and it’s such an incredible feeling to wake up every morning and see clearly without depending on glasses or contact lenses.

A heartfelt thank you to Dr. Aman Khanna, Dr. Ankita Khanna, and the entire team at Khanna Eye Centre for giving me the gift of clear vision. Thank you once again for helping me achieve a specs-free life! 😊
