Understanding ICL

What is ICL Surgery?

ICL stands for Implantable Collamer Lens. It is a type of phakic intraocular lens (IOL) that is implanted inside the eye, in front of the natural eye lens. Unlike LASIK, ICL surgery does not involve reshaping or removing tissue from the cornea.

The lens is made according to the measurements of your eye and implanted to help reduce your dependence on glasses. ICL can be an option for people who are not suitable candidates for LASIK and are looking for an alternative for vision correction.

ICL vs Contact Lenses

How is ICL different from contact lenses?

Contact lenses are a temporary form of vision correction and need to be applied and removed regularly. ICL, on the other hand, is an implanted lens that remains inside the eye and can offer high-definition vision, particularly in cases involving cylinder power.

Feature ICL Contact Lenses
Placement Implanted inside the eye Worn on the surface of the eye
Duration Long-term vision correction Temporary vision correction
Stability Does not move with blinking Can move during wear
Dryness Does not cause contact-lens-related dryness May cause dryness
Vision Quality High-definition vision Provides vision correction while worn

Questions Answered

ICL surgery FAQs

How can I book an appointment for ICL Surgery at Khanna Eye Centre Nirmaan Vihar?
You can contact Khanna Eye Centre Nirmaan Vihar to schedule an ICL consultation. The doctor will assess your eyes and determine whether ICL is suitable for you.
What is ICL eye surgery?
ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens) surgery is a vision correction procedure in which a specially designed lens is implanted inside the eye to correct refractive errors.
How is ICL surgery done?
A customised ICL lens is placed inside the eye through a small opening. The lens corrects the eye's focusing power without removing corneal tissue.
How much does ICL surgery in Delhi, India cost?
The cost of ICL surgery varies depending on factors such as the type of lens and individual eye requirements. A consultation can help determine the exact cost for your treatment.
What are the complications and risks associated with eye surgery?
Like any surgical procedure, ICL surgery may have certain risks or complications. Your eye specialist will discuss the potential risks and suitability of the procedure during your consultation.
What are the benefits of ICL surgery?
ICL can correct certain refractive errors while preserving the corneal tissue. It may also reduce dependence on glasses or contact lenses.
What is the procedure for ICL surgery?
The procedure involves detailed eye measurements, lens selection, and implantation of the customised ICL into the eye. Your doctor will provide instructions before and after surgery.
How long does ICL surgery take?
ICL surgery is a relatively short procedure and typically takes around 15–20 minutes.
What precautions should patients take after ICL surgery?
Patients should follow their surgeon's instructions, use prescribed medications as directed, and avoid activities that could irritate or injure the eyes during the initial recovery period.
What is the recovery time after ICL surgery?
Recovery varies between patients, but many can gradually return to their daily activities within a few days, as advised by their eye specialist.
How to correct blurry vision after ICL surgery?
Some temporary blurriness can occur during the initial recovery period. If blurry vision persists or worsens, consult your eye specialist for an examination.
For how long will I have to take medications after ICL surgery?
The duration of medication varies based on your individual recovery. Your surgeon will prescribe the required eye drops and tell you how long to use them.
What if my vision changes after I receive the ICL?
If your vision changes after ICL implantation, your eye specialist can assess the cause and recommend appropriate treatment or further correction if required.
What happens during ICL screening?
ICL screening involves detailed measurements and examinations of your eyes to assess your eye health, refractive error, and suitability for ICL surgery.
Will I ever get glasses after ICL treatment?
ICL can significantly reduce dependence on glasses, but some patients may still need glasses for certain activities or if their prescription changes over time.
Is ICL safer than LASIK?
ICL and LASIK have different benefits, risks, and suitability criteria. Your eye specialist can recommend the more appropriate option based on your eye health and measurements.
Can ICL correct high eye power or astigmatism?
ICL can correct high levels of myopia, and certain ICL lenses can also correct astigmatism. Your suitability depends on your individual eye measurements.
How long will the implanted lens last?
An ICL is designed for long-term implantation and can remain in the eye for many years. It can also be removed or replaced if medically necessary.
