Eye Care Tips

How to take care of your eyes daily?

By Khanna Eye Centre2 min read

Use UV-Protected Sunglasses

  • UV-rays from the sun can have significant dangerous effects on our eyes over prolonged exposure.
  • UV rays can affect our central vision, damaging the macula, which is main visual part of the retina in the back of the eye.
  • Several diseases and sight problems can result from exposure to the sun like macular degeneration, cataract, pterygium, corneal sunburn and skin cancer.
  • So one should wear sunglasses which block harmful UV-rays, to stay safe from the adverse effects caused by them.

Have a Healthy Diet

For a Healthy Eye, Eat Healthy.

Some nutrients like zinc, betacarotene, omega-3 fatty acids and Vitamins C and E are beneficial for you to maintain healthy sight as you get older. They may help to prevent age-related vision problems like cataracts and macular degeneration. A well-balanced eye diet can help you to stay at a healthy weight, lowering your cholesterol, decrease obesity issues and type 2 diabetes and other diseases that can cause vision problems.

Some of the foods for healthy eyes to include in your diet are:

  • Fish
  • Eggs
  • Green leafy vegetables

Avoid Smoking

  • Smoking is really bad for not only your overall health, but also very harmful for your eyes.
  • Various eye diseases like cataracts, glaucoma, age-related macular degeneration, and dry eye syndrome can be caused by smoking.
  • In fact, smoking is so bad for your health that heavy smokers are three times more likely to get cataracts than non-smokers.
  • So avoid cigarettes and smoking in your daily life, to have healthy eyes and suffer no health issues.

Reduce Eye Strain

Viewing at a computer or mobile screen for too long can cause discomfort known as “digital eye strain” that includes such symptoms as:

  1. blurry vision,
  2. trouble focusing at a distance,
  3. dry eyes,
  4. headaches.

This is known as “computer vision syndrome”. Follow the 20-20-20 rule.

For details refer to computer vision syndrome blog.

Get Regular Eye checkups!

Last but most important, remember to get regular eye check-ups done to ensure that any issues with your vision can be detected in its early stages.

A comprehensive eye test helps to ensure healthy eyes.
