Empanelments & Insurance Partners

Empanelments at Khanna Eye Centre

At Khanna Eye Centre, we are empanelled with various government organisations, Third-Party Administrators (TPAs), and insurance providers. This allows eligible patients to access eye care and surgical services under their applicable health insurance or organisational healthcare benefits.

Our empanelment network includes government panels and private TPAs. If you are covered under any of the listed organisations, our team can help you understand the applicable coverage, documentation, and cashless treatment process.

Process

How to Use Your Insurance or TPA Benefits?

If you are covered under one of our empanelled organisations, our team can guide you through the process.

1

Check your coverage

Confirm whether your insurance policy or healthcare scheme covers the required eye treatment.

2

Share your documents

Keep your insurance card, government health card, and other required documents ready.

3

Confirm eligibility

Our team can help verify your empanelment and the applicable treatment benefits.

4

Proceed with treatment

Once the required approvals are completed, you can proceed with your treatment as per the terms of your policy or scheme.

Need Help With Your Insurance Coverage?

Not sure whether your policy is accepted at Khanna Eye Centre? Our team can help you check your eligibility and understand the documentation required for your treatment.

Contact Our Team

Empanelment does not guarantee coverage for every treatment or procedure. Coverage, cashless facility, and approval are subject to the terms and conditions of the applicable insurance policy or healthcare scheme. Please contact Khanna Eye Centre for the latest information regarding your eligibility and treatment coverage.
