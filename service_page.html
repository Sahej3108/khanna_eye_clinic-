Understanding LASIK

What is LASIK?

LASIK is the short form for Laser-Assisted In-Situ Keratomileusis. It is a modern vision correction procedure that reshapes the cornea using laser technology so that light entering the eye focuses correctly on the retina.

Modern LASIK is safe, quick, and allows most patients to return home shortly after the treatment. It can permanently correct myopia (nearsightedness), hyperopia (farsightedness), and astigmatism.

Choosing your procedure

Which procedure to choose?

The most suitable vision correction procedure depends on your spectacle power, corneal measurements, and detailed eye evaluation.

Spectacle Power Range Recommended Procedure
-1 to -8 D Advanced Laser Procedures (LASIK / FEMTO LASIK / Customized LASIK)
-8 to -20 D Phakic IOL (ICL/IPCL)
+1 to +7 D Hyperopic LASIK / Advanced Laser Procedures
+5 to +10 D Phakic IOL (ICL/IPCL) or Refractive Lens Exchange (RLE)

*Final procedure selection is determined after detailed clinical evaluation and eye diagnostic tests to ensure the safest and most effective outcome.

Questions Answered

LASIK surgery FAQs

Who is not suitable for LASIK eye surgery?
People with unstable spectacle power, very thin corneas, certain eye diseases, or specific medical conditions may not be suitable. A detailed eye evaluation determines eligibility.
Is LASIK painful?
LASIK is generally painless because anesthetic eye drops are used before the procedure. Some patients may experience mild discomfort or irritation for a short time afterward.
Can LASIK correct all types of vision problems?
LASIK can correct myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism in suitable candidates. Some patients with very high power may require other procedures like ICL/IPCL.
Are there any side effects of LASIK?
Temporary symptoms such as dryness, glare, halos, or light sensitivity may occur after surgery. These usually improve as the eyes heal within a few weeks.
Can both eyes be treated together?
Yes, LASIK is commonly performed on both eyes during the same session. This allows faster visual recovery and greater convenience.
How much time does LASIK surgery take?
The laser procedure itself usually takes only a few minutes per eye. The complete visit, including preparation and observation, may take a few hours.
Is LASIK permanent?
LASIK permanently reshapes the cornea to correct vision. However, natural age-related changes in eyesight can still happen later in life.
How soon can I drive after LASIK?
Many patients can resume driving within a few days once vision becomes clear enough. Your doctor will advise based on your recovery progress.
Can I use eye makeup after surgery?
Eye makeup and cosmetics are usually avoided for a short period after surgery to reduce irritation and infection risk. Follow your doctor's specific instructions.
What is the worst possible complication?
Serious complications are rare, especially with modern technology and proper screening. Risks are discussed during evaluation, and safety measures are taken throughout treatment.
