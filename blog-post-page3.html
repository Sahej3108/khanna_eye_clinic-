Computer Vision Syndrome

By Khanna Eye Centre1 min read

Do you Experience?

  • Headache
  • Eye irritation
  • Blurry vision
  • Burning eyes
  • Excessive tearing
  • Eye fatigue
  • Frequent blinking
You might be suffering from Computer Vision Syndrome

A common problem with most of the computer users due to prolonged usage of computers

Tips to Remember

Monitor Location

Ideally more than 25 inches from eye and 6 inches below the horizontal eye level

Anti glare screen

Will decrease the amount of light reflected from the screen

20-20-20 rule

Take short breaks for your eyes every 20 minutes between your work for 20 seconds then look at objects that are at least 20 feet away

Eye Exercise

Close your eyes and slowly roll your eyeballs clockwise and anticlockwise. Repeat it 3 times.

Light Effect

Use suspended lights from ceiling and windows with shades, blinds and curtains. Avoid light hitting directly on eyes.

Sitting Posture

Use chairs that provide support while typing, position of head slightly tilted downwards and height of the chair adjusted so that feet rest flat on the floor.

Blinking

Blink frequently to keep the front surface of your eyes moist.

A.C. distance

Avoid sitting at a location with direct blow of air on eyes from blower or an AC or for a long period in a room with low humidity.
